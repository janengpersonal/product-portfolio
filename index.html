Context

Alpha is a job simulation platform that helps recruiters and line managers assess a candidate's real-world capabilities. Candidates complete role-specific scenarios that mirror actual job tasks, demonstrating their qualifications through action rather than self-reported claims. Once submitted, an AI-integrated evaluation engine scores each submission and generates a competency breakdown aligned to the role's requirements. The result: recruiters and line managers get a clear, evidence-based view of what a candidate can actually do — not just what their resume says.

(PRD) & Dashboard UX & Acceptance Criteria

Problem Statement

Recruiters often rely heavily on CVs and manual assessments to identify qualified candidates. This creates a time-consuming screening process and makes it difficult to validate whether candidates can actually perform the skills required for a role.

The Recruitment Dashboard enables recruiters to manage the hiring pipeline and use job simulations as an evidence-based assessment layer alongside CV information.

Product goals

  • Reduce manual CV and assessment screening effort.
  • Help recruiters identify qualified candidates faster using simulation-based evidence.
  • Provide a centralized view of candidates, scores, competencies, and hiring stages.
  • Enable recruiters to manage simulation invitations and candidate progression.

Non-goals for MVP

  • Fully automated hiring decisions without human review.
  • Automated interview scheduling and interview evaluation.
  • Automated candidate rejection based solely on AI score.
  • Building a full ATS replacement.
  • Candidate-view dashboard.

Primary users

TA/ Recruiter

  • Create and manage job roles.
  • View candidates’ simulation results, competencies and status.
  • Shortlist candidates.
  • Send simulation invitations.

Hiring Manager

  • View shortlisted candidates.
  • View candidates’ results, competencies analysis as evidence.
  • Provide feedback.

MVP scope

AI supports the decision-making process but does not replace human-approval.

V1- ships within initial 6-week window

  • Funnel overview (Applied vs. Shortlisted vs. Interviewing vs. Offered).
  • Candidate list with AI score, filterable by role, score range, and completion status.
  • Candidate Detail View with competency breakdown and evidence.
  • "Send simulation invitation" action.
  • Basic role-based access control (TA vs. Hiring Manager).
  • CSV export of shortlist.

Later phase V2+

  • PDF export with branded formatting.
  • ATS integration (auto-sync candidate status back to customer's existing ATS).
  • Email/calendar integration for interview scheduling.
  • SSO for enterprise customers.
  • Advanced analytics tiles (time-to-fill, completion-rate trends over time, benchmarking across roles).
  • "Flow Manager" self-serve UI for customers to reorder assessment vs. interview steps.

Functional requirements

CategoryRequirements
Job & Funnel Management
  • FR1: Recruiter can create a job posting and associate it with one Job Simulation.
  • FR2: Recruiter can choose the hiring flow (CV-first or simulation-first) per job posting.
  • FR3: Dashboard displays a funnel view showing candidate counts at each stage (Applied, Simulation Sent, Simulation Completed, Shortlisted, Interviewing, Offered, Rejected).
  • FR4: Recruiter can view a list of all open jobs with status and applicant counts.
Candidate Management
  • FR5: Recruiter can view a candidate list per job, showing name, CV match score, AI assessment score, simulation completion status, and current stage.
  • FR6: Recruiter can filter the candidate list by role, score range, simulation completion status, and pipeline stage.
  • FR7: System automatically sends a simulation invitation to any candidate whose CV match score meets or exceeds the configured threshold (default 70%); recruiter can also send invitations manually, individually or in bulk, to candidates below the threshold.
  • FR8: Recruiter can open a Candidate Detail View to see full simulation results, competency breakdown, and reviewer notes.
  • FR9: Recruiter can add notes and advance/reject a candidate from within the Candidate Detail View.
  • FR9a: System automatically flags a candidate as eligible to advance to interview when their assessment score meets or exceeds the configured threshold (default 70%); this is a recommendation surfaced to the recruiter, not an automatic stage change — the recruiter makes the final advance/reject decision (see Non-Goals: no fully automated hiring decisions).
Reporting & Alerts
  • FR10: Dashboard surfaces "Action required" alerts (e.g., simulation completed and awaiting review, candidate stalled at a stage for X days, candidate crossed the eligibility threshold and is awaiting a decision).
  • FR11: Recruiter can export a shortlist report as CSV.
  • FR12: Dashboard displays basic analytics tiles: completion rate and time-to-fill proxy per role.
Configuration
  • FR13: Recruiter or admin can configure the CV-match and assessment-score thresholds per role/customer; system defaults to 70% for both if left unset.
Access Control
  • FR14: System enforces role-based access — TA/Recruiter has full edit access to their assigned requisitions; Hiring Manager has read access plus feedback/comment ability on shortlisted candidates only.

Non-functional requirements

  • Security: All candidate data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Access strictly scoped by role and requisition assignment.
  • Privacy: Candidate data retention policy configurable per enterprise customer; candidates can request data deletion in line with applicable data-protection regulations.
  • Audit logging: Every recruiter action that changes a candidate's stage, score visibility, or access permissions is logged with actor, timestamp, and before/after state.
  • Latency: Dashboard views (funnel, candidate list) must load within 2 seconds for pipelines of up to 500 candidates.
  • Availability: 99.5% uptime target for the dashboard during customer business hours.
  • Bias mitigation: AI scoring methodology must be documented and available for recruiter/customer review to support fairness audits.

Integration (v2)

  • ATS export: push shortlisted candidates and status updates to customer's existing ATS (e.g., Greenhouse, Lever).
  • Email/calendar: trigger interview scheduling once a candidate is advanced.
  • SSO: enterprise login via customer's identity provider.

Data Logic

For each completed Attempt, the dashboard needs the simulation engine to return:

  • Time spent on the simulation overall and per task, to flag unusually fast/slow completions for review.
  • Accuracy/quality metrics relevant to the simulation type (e.g., code correctness, task completion rate).
  • Soft-skill scoring (e.g., communication, problem-solving approach) where the simulation includes interactive or written components.
  • Competency sub-scores mapped to the role's required skills, so the dashboard can render the competency breakdown shown in the Candidate Detail View.
  • Raw evidence artifacts (outputs, transcripts) that a recruiter can open for manual verification of the AI score.

Acceptance Criteria

User story 1: As a recruiter, I want candidates to demonstrate their skills through realistic job simulations, so I can evaluate their capabilities beyond their CV

Given a candidate has been invited to complete a job simulation

When the candidate clicks the simulation link in the invitation email

Then the system directs the candidate to the authentication screen

And after successful authentication, the candidate is directed to the assigned simulation.

Given a candidate's answer has reached the 2,000-character limit

When the candidate attempts to enter another character

Then the system prevents the answer from exceeding 2,000 characters

And displays an appropriate validation message.

Given the candidate has completed all required simulation questions

When the candidate clicks Submit

Then the system saves the candidate's submission

And sends the submission to the AI scoring process

And updates the simulation status to In Review

And prevents the candidate from editing the submitted answers.

Given a candidate has submitted a simulation and AI scoring is in progress

When the recruiter opens the candidate's profile

Then the simulation status is displayed as In Review

And the recruiter cannot view the final AI-generated score until scoring is complete.

Given the simulation status is Completed

When a recruiter with the appropriate permission opens the candidate's simulation results

Then the recruiter can view the overall score and competency breakdown mapped to the job requirements

And the recruiter can use these results as evidence when evaluating the candidate.

User story 2: As a recruiter, I want to use an automated job simulation to assess candidates’ functional skills so that I can evaluate more candidates with less manual effort.

Given a candidate has completed and submitted the job simulation

When the submission is received by the system

Then the system automatically evaluates the candidate's functional skills based on the competencies defined for the selected role.

Given the system has completed evaluating a candidate's simulation

When the scoring process is completed

Then the system generates an overall simulation score and competency-level scores for the candidate

And stores the results in the candidate's assessment record.

Given multiple candidates have completed the simulation and their results are available

When a recruiter views the completed candidates for a selected role

Then the system displays their assessment scores and competency breakdowns in the recruitment dashboard

And allows the recruiter to compare candidates without manually reviewing and scoring each submission.
